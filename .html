ABOUT THE PANCHAYAT

Understanding Pallippuram

Pallippuram Grama Panchayat is located in Ernakulam district, Kerala. Its geographical setting, settlements and local resources are important for understanding the area and supporting local-level planning.

This WebGIS website provides a digital platform to explore local geographic information and maps.
